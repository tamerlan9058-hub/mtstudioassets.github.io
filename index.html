Graphics

From phones to gaming PCs

One world, five graphics levels. Players pick one in the settings or let the game detect it.

  • Volumetric clouds with their own shadows, light shafts and fog
  • Water that flows, reflects the shore and lights the sea floor
  • Far terrain to the horizon past the loaded chunks
  • Block light from torches and lamps, ambient occlusion
  • Visual styles, from cinematic to stylized
Gameplay

A survival loop out of the box

Mine, build, craft at a table, survive the night. Every part is data you can change in the inspector.

  • Inventory with drag and drop, on mouse and touch
  • Crafting stations that bring their own recipes
  • Mobs with moving parts: animals by day, zombies at night
  • Main menu, worlds with seeds, saving and loading
  • Mobile touch controls and UI from editable UXML layouts
